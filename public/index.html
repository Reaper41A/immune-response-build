HOW TO PLAY
1. Move
On touch, drag anywhere on the left half of the screen like a joystick. On desktop use WASD or the arrow keys.
2. Fire — targeting is automatic
Touch: drag the right-side aim stick — past the small dead zone it fires toward wherever you point it. Desktop: move the mouse to aim, hold left-click or SPACE to fire. Aim assist (Settings) gently nudges your shot toward a nearby enemy when you're close but not quite on target — it never fires on its own or picks a target for you. Each class has a different range; watch the dashed ring while firing. An optional tracer line (Settings → Controls) shows exactly where you're aiming.

3. Use your ability
Touch: drag your fire-stick past the outer dashed ring to trigger your class ability (Floating scheme), or tap the ability button locked above your aim stick (Fixed scheme — Settings → Controls). Desktop: press E, or tap the blue button above FIRE. Abilities never block movement or firing — use it whenever it's glowing and off cooldown.
Finding yourself
You always have the magenta ring + chevron and the label "YOU" — even if a squadmate picks the same class as you.

The goal & leaks
Stop pathogens before they touch the central Body. Every leak drains shared BODY HP and chips one random organ — Heart (slower move), Lungs (longer ability cooldowns) or Brain (less damage). At 0 organ HP that debuff stays for the whole run.
🧬
Between waves — three drafts
Squad upgrade: one shared pick bought with squad EP — whoever confirms buys their highlighted card; bot votes are advice, not law.
Personal perk: free pick, just for you.
Ability evolution every 3 waves: upgrades only YOUR ability. All choices are timed.
🔥
Ammo & heat
Shots cost ammo and build heat. Green motes dropped by kills refill ammo — walk over them. Hit max heat and your weapon overheats and jams briefly. Both fully reset between waves.
